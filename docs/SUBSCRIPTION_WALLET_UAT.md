## Implemented contract

- Subscription plan governance is service-agnostic for Grooming, Dog Training, Boarding, Pet Sitting, Dog Walking and Pet Taxi, with city/service pricing, session counts and validity windows.
- Plan creation and update both enforce the core commercial invariants: non-negative finite price, at least one session, at least one validity unit, and validity measured only in days or months.
- The canonical Grooming subscription wallet remains the implemented credit ledger: one `customer_grooming_subscriptions` account owns total, reserved and consumed credits, and one `booking_subscription_usage` row links a booking to its reserved/consumed/released credits.
- Available credits are `total - reserved - consumed` and never intentionally go below zero.
- Reserve requires a canonical Grooming booking for the same customer and sufficient available credits.
- Reservation is guarded so concurrent requests cannot both spend the same available credit.
- Consume is permitted only after canonical booking completion; release is permitted only while the service is not completed.
- Wallet mutations are idempotent and audited.
- Pause uses only the plan-configured pause entitlement and requires a reason. Pause does not invent an expiry extension; expiry extension remains `policy_required` until approved.
- Expiry respects the configured grace period. Renewal readiness may be surfaced, but auto-renewal is disabled and renewal pricing remains `configuration_required`.
- In LIVE payment mode, a customer-submitted payment state does not activate entitlement. Usable subscription credits are granted only after a verified matching gateway capture, and duplicate gateway events do not duplicate credits.
- A verified payment failure leaves no usable subscription entitlement.
- The wallet API itself does not initiate live renewal/refund money movement.

## Staff/live UAT still required before production sign-off

1. Load a canonical UAT subscription and verify total/reserved/consumed/available balances.
2. Attempt a reservation for another customer's booking and confirm denial.
3. Reserve one credit for a valid future Grooming booking; retry idempotently and confirm no duplicate movement.
4. Attempt to over-reserve and confirm rejection.
5. Attempt consumption before booking completion and confirm rejection.
6. Complete the canonical Grooming service and verify exactly-once consumption.
7. Reserve against a cancellable booking, cancel it through the governed cancellation flow, then verify the credit is released correctly.
8. Pause within configured entitlement; try exceeding entitlement and confirm rejection; resume.
9. Validate expiry, grace and renewal-due calculations with controlled UAT dates/data.
10. Confirm customer ownership, staff permissions and security audit records.
11. In the configured LIVE gateway environment, prove one real/sandbox-approved gateway capture activates entitlement once and a failed/invalid verification grants zero credits.
12. Confirm no automated renewal charge or automated refund settlement is generated by the wallet flow.

## Intentionally not claimed by this closure

- Automated recurring renewal charging or renewal discounts.
- Upgrade/downgrade commercial rules.
- Pause-related validity extension policy beyond the configured behavior.
- Automated cash refund settlement for subscription purchases.
- A mixed-service credit wallet: plan governance is cross-service, while the implemented canonical credit wallet is still Grooming-specific.
- Final production accounting/GST reconciliation and human/device UAT.

These items require approved PawSpace policy or separate implementation/UAT before they can be called production-complete.
